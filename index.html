Mechanics library

MECHANICS

Reload discipline

Reload when you have safe time and cover, not automatically after every fight. Track ammo before committing.

PEEKING

Jiggle peeking

Use a brief shoulder peek to gather information, then return to cover. Avoid repeating the same timing.

PEEKING

Re-peeking with purpose

After showing your position, change the timing or angle before challenging again. Do not offer an easy repeat shot.

POSITIONING

Slicing the pie

Clear angles in small sections while keeping as much cover as possible. Avoid exposing yourself to several lines at once.

MOVEMENT

Move with a reason

Choose movement that supports your next position. Pause when information matters and avoid unnecessary noise.

GUNFIGHTS

Isolate engagements

Use cover, teammate information and timing to reduce the number of angles you must manage at once.

Weekly practice template

  • Review a short section of your own gameplay and identify one repeated mistake.
  • Practise one mechanic deliberately in a low-pressure mode.
  • In matches, focus on one decision-making goal rather than trying to change everything at once.
  • At the end, note what improved and what to work on next.

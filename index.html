STARTER LESSON SET All Game sense Teamwork Preparation Review GAME SENSEFOUNDATION Make a plan before committing Turn each round into a sequence of small decisions instead of reacting without a plan. Open step-by-step lesson 1 Identify the round objective and your team's immediate priority. 2 Gather the information available to you before choosing a route or action. 3 Agree who is doing what so teammates do not duplicate effort. 4 Pause when new information changes the situation, then adapt. 5 After the round, review whether the plan matched what happened. PREPARATIONFOUNDATION Build a repeatable warm-up Use a short, consistent routine to settle in and focus on one learning goal. Open step-by-step lesson 1 Choose one skill to focus on for the session. 2 Spend a few minutes in a suitable practice mode learning the controls and feel. 3 Move into a low-pressure match and apply that one skill. 4 Notice one thing that improved and one thing to revisit. 5 Keep the routine comfortable and take breaks when needed. TEAMWORKCOMMUNICATION Give useful, concise callouts Make communication clear, calm and actionable for your teammates. Open step-by-step lesson 1 Lead with the most useful information you know. 2 Use the game's recognised map callout where you know it accurately. 3 Keep the message short and avoid guessing when uncertain. 4 Update teammates if the information changes. 5 Leave space for others to communicate. REVIEWIMPROVEMENT Review a round without blaming yourself Use match review to find one useful lesson rather than judging the whole performance. Open step-by-step lesson 1 Choose one moment you want to understand better. 2 Write down what information you had at that moment. 3 List the options you noticed at the time. 4 Identify what you would try differently next time. 5 Practise that single adjustment in a future session. GAME SENSEDECISION-MAKING Recognise when to adapt A good plan is flexible. New information should change your next decision. Open step-by-step lesson 1 Notice when the round no longer matches your original expectation. 2 Separate confirmed information from assumptions. 3 Tell teammates what has changed. 4 Choose a new objective that fits the current situation. 5 Review whether the adjustment helped the team. PREPARATIONLEARNING Learn one map area at a time Build map knowledge in manageable chunks instead of trying to memorise everything at once. Open step-by-step lesson 1 Choose one map and one floor or area to study. 2 Learn the room names and the routes shown by the in-game map. 3 Check how the area connects to nearby rooms. 4 Practise recognising the area in a few matches. 5 Move to another area when the first feels familiar.
